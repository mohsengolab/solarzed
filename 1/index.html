GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

What does my warranty cover?

Coverage, exclusions and duration depend on the terms issued by your film supplier or installer. This demo does not set or verify those terms.

Can I find my record on another device?

Not in this demo. Records stay in this browser’s local storage. Clearing browser data removes them. A production portal needs a shared database and secure access.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Contact the installer or warranty issuer listed on your original documents. Keep your invoice, film serial number and installation record available.
