# Proposed Solarzed warranty policy v1.0

Source: the Persian policy supplied by the user on 2026-10-23.

This update implements the presentation of Film Warranty, Installation Warranty and Digital Warranty and an English customer-facing summary of the proposed terms. It does not activate coverage or select any proposed duration.

Existing functionality remains installation registration, SQL Server storage, lookup and optional seller SMS adapter. These are not a complete implementation of the proposed warranty operations.

Still required for an operational policy rollout: installer sign-in, roll and batch inventory, approved product-specific film and installation durations, country and privacy workflow, verification and pending approval states, human review for duplicate or incomplete data, certificate issuance and coverage dates, claims with separate IDs and evidence, access-controlled service history, review decisions and administrative reports. Existing duplicate protection is a technical guard only; it does not implement the policy's manual-review pathway and must not be presented as a final claim decision.

The two-day, three-day and seven-day targets are labeled proposed service targets. Final coverage, costs and country-specific terms require approval. Internal supplier identities and management data are not published on the customer page.
