PROPOSED POLICY / VERSION 1.0

This is Solarzed’s proposed commercial warranty framework. Final terms require product-specific approval and review for each market. Commercial terms do not restrict statutory consumer rights.

IN THIS POLICYFilm warrantyInstallation warrantyDigital certificateRegistration & activationOriginal customerClaims & reviewAvailable remediesCommon questions
01 / FILM WARRANTY

The quality of the product.

For confirmed defects in the film under normal use and the manufacturer’s care instructions. Examples may include abnormal discoloration, delamination or bubbling attributable to the product, subject to approved technical criteria and exclusions.

Film coverage duration must be set separately for each model. A uniform duration is not assumed for all rolls.

02 / INSTALLATION WARRANTY

The quality of the workmanship.

For verified installation issues such as unacceptable trapped contamination, poor trimming or execution-related edge lifting, assessed against the instructions for the installed model.

Installation coverage has its own duration and conditions. Registration alone does not prove that an installation is free from defects.

03 / DIGITAL WARRANTY

A certificate that connects the details.

The proposed digital certificate links the product model, traceable roll identifier, vehicle, installation date and center, original customer reference, warranty status and service history.

Film coverage start and endInstallation coverage start and endWarranty ID and lookup linkService request and full terms

These certificate fields describe the target policy. Unapproved periods must not be displayed as active coverage. Supplier identities, purchase documents and internal supply-chain data are not published on the public certificate unless disclosure is legally required.

04 / REGISTRATION & ACTIVATION

Recorded first. Verified before activation.

Installers can submit a request. Activation depends on verifying the serial, product model, approved batch, installation details and original customer information. Requests needing human review remain pending approval.

  1. Identify the roll and its production batch.
  2. Scan or enter the unique product serial.
  3. Record the vehicle, country, installer, date and selected glass.
  4. Provide necessary customer details with appropriate privacy information.
  5. Check serial status, batch consistency and earlier registrations.
  6. Issue the certificate after verification is complete.

A duplicate serial or inconsistent record is a review signal, not proof of fraud. The customer or installer should be able to correct an error or provide alternative evidence.

05 / THE ORIGINAL CUSTOMER

A clear link to the first installation.

The proposed commercial warranty is issued to the original customer. A change in vehicle ownership does not by itself transfer that commercial warranty, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Missing receipts, incomplete information or registration by a non-authorized installer must not automatically reject every claim. Alternative evidence and manual review should be available as required for the market.

A supplier change alone does not cancel an existing valid warranty. Solarzed remains responsible for its commitments to the customer.

06 / CLAIMS & TECHNICAL REVIEW

Every concern deserves a clear process.

Submit a description of the issue and initial photos through the support channel or installation center. Eligibility depends on the relevant coverage, timing, evidence and technical assessment, while preserving statutory rights.

These are proposed operational targets, not guaranteed legal deadlines. Shorter statutory deadlines take precedence. If additional inspection is needed, the customer should be informed of the reason and revised estimate.

What may be outside commercial coverage

Collision, scratches, impact, unsuitable chemicals, damage caused by later modifications, abnormal use or unrelated glass and vehicle defects may fall outside commercial coverage when supported by the final terms and evidence. A claim must not be dismissed simply by labeling it misuse; the cause should be reasonably assessed and documented.

07 / REMEDIES & COSTS

A remedy appropriate to the cause.

For accepted claims, the appropriate remedy may include correcting the installation, replacing the affected film, reinstalling an equivalent product or a refund or other remedy where the terms or applicable law require it.

Labor, removal, refitting, transport and related costs must be defined in the approved warranty terms. No unconditional promise of free removal, replacement or labor is made by this proposed policy.

Responsibility for product and workmanship issues is determined by the cause, service contracts and applicable law.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few clear answers.

How long is the warranty?

Film and installation periods are approved separately for each model. The proposed policy does not select a fixed duration for all products.

Does registration mean my warranty is active?

No. Product and installation information must be checked before activation. The current installation confirmation is a record of submission, not proof of approved coverage.

Can the warranty move to the next vehicle owner?

The proposed commercial warranty is for the original customer, subject to any mandatory local rights for subsequent owners or consumers.

What if I do not have the original receipt?

Alternative evidence and manual review should be available. A missing receipt must not automatically reject every claim.

What happens if the supplier changes?

A supplier change alone does not invalidate a prior valid warranty. Solarzed’s customer commitments continue to apply.
